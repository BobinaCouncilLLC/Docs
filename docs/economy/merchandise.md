Official Bobina merchandise with holder benefits and Web3 checkout.

Browse and purchase exclusive Bobina merchandise directly on our platform at [bobina.moe/merch](https://bobina.moe/merch). We offer a seamless Web3-native shopping experience with special perks for $BOBINA holders.

### Payment Options

- Pay with **ETH** — settlement chain varies per season (Ethereum, Base, or Ink)
- Pay with **$BOBINA** tokens on the season's settlement chain
- Select drops may support **partner token** payments (e.g. $BOBO, $MONKE, $CHIZU, $CHEESE, etc...)
- Wallet connection required — MetaMask, Coinbase Wallet, Rabby, and more supported

### Holder Tier Benefits

$BOBINA holders receive **automatic discounts** at checkout based on their holder tier. No codes needed — just connect your verified wallet and save.

| Tier | Min. Holdings | Merch Discount |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 🐝 Busy Bee | 10,000 $BOBINA (0.10%) | 5% off |
| 🐻 Bearsexual | 25,000 $BOBINA (0.25%) | 7% off |
| 🍯 Honey | 50,000 $BOBINA (0.50%) | 12% off |
| 💋 Bobinachad | 100,000 $BOBINA (1.00%) | 20% off |

Tier discounts apply to ETH, $BOBINA, and partner token prices. Your tier is detected automatically via your verified companion wallet.

### Order Fulfillment

All merchandise is printed on-demand and ships worldwide through our fulfillment partners.

- Production time: 2-5 business days
- Shipping: 5-10 business days (varies by location)
- Tracking information provided once shipped

### Proceeds

Bobina Council LLC may use all proceeds at its sole discretion, with no commitment to buy back tokens. **$BOBINA** follows the same rules as every other token (Terms sections 2.7 and 2.8). There is no buyback commitment.

Every purchase supports the Bobina Council ecosystem while you get awesome gear!
