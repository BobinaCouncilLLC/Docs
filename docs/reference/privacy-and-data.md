What we collect, how we protect you, and your rights.

## Login Providers

Bobina Council supports multiple OAuth login providers. You can sign in with any of the following:

- **X (Twitter)** - Display name, username, profile picture, user ID
- **Google** - Display name, email, profile picture, account ID
- **TikTok** - Display name, profile picture, open ID
- **Facebook** - Display name, profile picture, user ID

## No Passwords Stored

Bobina Council uses OAuth exclusively for authentication. We **never collect, store, transmit, or have access to your passwords**. Your login credentials are managed entirely by your chosen provider (X, Google, TikTok, Discord, Telegram, or wallet). Authentication tokens are handled server-side and never exposed to the client.

## Data We Collect

- **Profile Data:** Display name, profile picture, and platform user ID from your login provider
- **Council Profile:** Your chosen username, companion name, profile slug (e.g. bobina.moe/yourname)
- **Companion Data:** Conversation history, relationship settings, memories, and interaction patterns with Bobina
- **Voting & Contributions:** Your votes, submitted Bobinas, and governance participation
- **Platform IDs:** Immutable identifiers from each provider, used to recognize returning users

## Deleting Conversational Data

You can delete individual conversations with Bobina from the [Terminal Companion tab](https://bobina.moe/?terminal=companion). Open the menu, go to History, and tap the trash icon on any conversation. Deleted conversations are permanently removed from our companion servers and cannot be recovered. You may delete any or all of your conversational data at any time.

## Account Deletion

You can deactivate your account from the [Terminal Settings](https://bobina.moe/?terminal=settings) (Danger tab). Disconnecting removes:

- **Removed:** Your Council Profile and public profile page (your profile slug)
- **Removed:** Your active session and login state

The following is preserved:

- **Preserved:** Submitted Bobinas, votes, and contributions
- **Preserved:** Companion data (conversations, memories, relationship settings)
- **Preserved:** Immutable platform IDs for account restoration

If you log in again with the same provider account, your preserved data is automatically restored. You can then delete any remaining data (like conversation history) you wish to remove.

> **Warning:** **Permanent deletion:** For complete removal of all data including research data, contact [contact@bobina.moe](mailto:contact@bobina.moe). Due to usage of interaction data for research purposes, permanent deletion requires manual review.

## Research & Development

We reserve the right to analyze interaction data with Bobina for research and development. This includes behavioral patterns, voting metrics, and communication logs. This data improves Bobina's capabilities and informs new features. Research data may be retained after account disconnection. See our full [Privacy Policy](https://bobina.moe/privacy-policy) for details.
