Your unified command center for all Bobina Council features. Access the Terminal from anywhere via your profile menu.

The Bobina Terminal is a full-screen modal HUD that provides access to all companion features, council statistics, and community tools in one unified interface. Open it by clicking your profile icon after signing in, or it will automatically open after completing onboarding.

### Overview

Bobina World Order global statistics, your personal relationship score, linked Discord/Telegram status, and quick stats on council activity.

### Companion

Chat with Bobina directly in the Terminal. Adjust all of your Companion Account settings easily, manage your conversation history, choose outfits for Bobina, and purchase credits with $BOBINA.

### Messages

View recent transmissions, achivements, role unlocks, and special messages from the Bobina Council in your own personal inbox.

### Tokens

Token call leaderboard showing top community predictions, call history, and market sentiment tracking via votes from human Council Members.

### Leaderboard

The Terminal tab is named **Leaderboard** (it used to be called Community). It shows community leaderboard statistics and fun metrics within the Bobina Council.

The tab address is still [`?terminal=community`](https://bobina.moe/?terminal=community). [`?terminal=leaderboard`](https://bobina.moe/?terminal=leaderboard) opens the same tab.

The homepage Leaderboard button, the Terminal's Leaderboard card, and [bobina.moe/leaderboard](https://bobina.moe/leaderboard) all open this tab. They used to open Tokens.

Routing:

* [bobina.moe/leaderboard?terminal=…](https://bobina.moe/leaderboard?terminal=overview) still opens whichever tab you name.
* A `/leaderboard` link with `?token=` or `?symbol=` still opens the Tokens tab (a specific token).
* `?terminal=leaderboard` lands on the Leaderboard tab.
* `/leaderboard/` with a trailing slash, in any capitalization (like `/LEADERBOARD/`), goes straight to the Leaderboard tab in one redirect.
* Old links to `/?terminal=community` keep working.

Any bobina.moe address that ends in a slash, like `bobina.moe/docs/`, redirects once to the same address without the slash.

### Roles & Unlocks

Learn about Council Roles and all of the achivements you can unlock using our platform.

### Records

Historical archive of approved Bobina and Council Proposals. These Records are very dear to the Bobina Council because they represent users like you who shape the future of our platform with your God-given right to vote. Thank you. 💖

### Challenges

Complete achievements and challenges to earn rewards. Track your progress across voting, contributing, companion interaction, and community engagement goals. Use them to customize your appearance throughout the Council.

### Settings

Manage your account, customize your profile appearance, connect Discord/Telegram, set profile themes, manage wallet connections, and access account controls including deactivation. Requires sign-in.

### Onboarding

First-time setup experience for new members. Create your profile, pick a username, customize your avatar, and get introduced to the Bobina Council. Automatically shown for new users.

## Terminal Deep Links

Share direct links to specific Terminal sections. Add `?terminal=` to any bobina.moe URL to open the Terminal automatically on that tab. Click any button below to try it!

| Tab | Deep link |
| --- | --- |
| `overview` | [`?terminal=overview`](https://bobina.moe/?terminal=overview) |
| `companion` | [`?terminal=companion`](https://bobina.moe/?terminal=companion) |
| `messages` | [`?terminal=messages`](https://bobina.moe/?terminal=messages) |
| `tokens` | [`?terminal=tokens`](https://bobina.moe/?terminal=tokens) |
| Leaderboard (`community`) | [`?terminal=community`](https://bobina.moe/?terminal=community) · [`?terminal=leaderboard`](https://bobina.moe/?terminal=leaderboard) |
| `roles` | [`?terminal=roles`](https://bobina.moe/?terminal=roles) |
| `records` | [`?terminal=records`](https://bobina.moe/?terminal=records) |
| `challenges` | [`?terminal=challenges`](https://bobina.moe/?terminal=challenges) |
| `settings` | [`?terminal=settings`](https://bobina.moe/?terminal=settings) |
| `onboarding` | [`?terminal=onboarding`](https://bobina.moe/?terminal=onboarding) |

**Token Deep Links:** Use `?token=CONTRACT_ADDRESS` or `?symbol=TICKER` to open the Terminal directly to a specific token detail page.

**Tip:** Click the glowing pink dot in the Terminal header to close it, or press Escape. The Terminal preserves your current tab when you reopen it.
